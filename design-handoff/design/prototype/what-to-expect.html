Experience

What to expect

Starting therapy takes three steps. You will know what each one involves before it begins.

  1. 01

    Step 1 of 3

    Consultation

    A short call with the clinician you chose. You share what brings you in; they explain how they work. If it is not the right fit, they will help you find someone who is.

    Length
    15 minutes
    Format
    Phone or video
    Cost
    Free
  2. 02

    Step 2 of 3

    Assessment

    Your first full session. We review your history, what has helped and what has not, and agree on clear goals. You leave with a written plan.

    Length
    60–75 minutes
    Format
    In person or online
    Before
    Intake forms, sent by email
  3. 03

    Step 3 of 3

    The work

    Regular sessions at a consistent time. Early on, weekly is best. We review progress together and adjust the plan as you change.

    Length
    45–60 minutes
    Frequency
    Weekly or every other week
    Reviews
    Every [8] sessions
