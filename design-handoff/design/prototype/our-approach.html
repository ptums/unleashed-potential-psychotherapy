Our Mission

Our approach

We provide careful, evidence-based behavioral health care, so more people can live without being governed by what happened to them.

Photo: quiet waiting room, morning light, wood and linen

Values

What guides our work

Evidence first
We use methods with research behind them, and we explain why.
Dignity
You are treated as the expert on your own life.
Steadiness
A consistent clinician, a consistent time, and a clear plan.
Access
In person or online, with transparent fees.

Standard of care

What every client can count on

  • Licensed clinicians only. Every therapist holds an active state license.
  • Trauma-informed care. We move at a pace you can manage.
  • A written treatment plan. Built with you and reviewed every [8] sessions.
  • Clinical consultation. Clinicians review cases together, with your privacy protected.
  • Private by default. HIPAA-compliant records and video.
